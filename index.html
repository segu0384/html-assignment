About Me

My name is Aimee Seguin. It is my first year at Algonquin College. I initially planned to take a 4 year program, Digital Marketing. It was cancelled last minute and I ended up having to choose another course. So here I am at IMD! I am enjoying this course so far and it excites me to think that I am one step closer to my future. I am not sure where exactly I see myself working in the future, but I'd love to have a job where I can work remotely and be able to travel the world.

I still remember the time Covid-19 hit... Back when the pandemic started, I was in 6th grade. I ended up spending my 7th and 8th year in online school. I met a lot of friends during my 8th year. Unfortuntely, pretty much all of those friends did not go to the same school as me when we finished 8th grade. ...Except for one. One person actually stuck with me, 4 years after 8th grade, we finally got to meet in person at AC! It is funny to me how we met each other online during middle school, and then finally got to see each other in person only after graduating high school.
